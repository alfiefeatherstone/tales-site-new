# Episode 10 - One More Time

**The Narrator:**  
Greetings my dear, loving listeners and welcome back to Tales Beneath the Surface. I do hope you’ve been keeping what I said last time in mind. I have and, if I’m as honest with you as I always am, it’s troubled my dreams and waking hours. I just can’t shake the feeling that there’s something I’m missing…it’s resting there…right at the tip of my tongue..well I’m sure it’ll show up as soon I stop thinking about it, you know the feeling! Resigning yourself to staying indoors because you can’t find your favourite pair of shoes, then looking down, and realising you’re wearing them! Wait and what’s that there? A trail of mud or something more foul?! My daydreamed steps must have wandered far from the beaten path, still any exercise is better than none, even if you can’t remember it!  

Speaking it out loud really makes you notice the power of words, doesn’t it? …I’m sure at least half of you are certain that I’ve traipsed *said with sudden anger* all manner of filth over my snug studio space…*calming* well have I?! *pause* You will never know, how could you? You’ll have to take my word for it…though I’m sure the thought will linger, like a tooth steadily giving into decay. The power of words really is something, isn’t it. Particularly when you can’t see the speaker, their movements and energy masked behind a sound system pouring liquid gold down your ear canals and into the ridged crevasses hiding in your skull…
