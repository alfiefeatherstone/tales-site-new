Bryony Purdue is a London-based singer, voiceover artist, songwriter, producer and creative producer whose career spans over a decade of performance, collaboration and community arts work. Originally from near the East Coast of Scotland, she grew up in an environment steeped in eclectic musical influences.  

She graduated from Trinity Laban Conservatoire of Music and Dance in 2017 with a BMus (Hons) in Classical Performance (Voice). With international tours spanning over ten years, including original headline shows from Edinburgh to Somerset, Bryony has developed a sound that melds elements of 70s pop, blues, rock and trip-hop. Her debut album [Dripfeed](https://orcd.co/xode98m), released on 31 October 2025,  is the culmination of years of work across music, theatre and cabaret.  

Beyond her solo work, Bryony has been a key figure in the Citizens of the World Choir, joining the team as freelance Creative Producer, having known the Citizens team for over a decade. [Citizensoftheworldchoir](https://www.citizensoftheworldchoir.org/meet-the-team) In 2025, she sang on Ed Sheeran's tracks Azizam and Sapphire and opened Glastonbury Festival on the Pyramid Stage alongside the Citizens of the World Choir.  bryony_

She is also Co-Founder and Executive Director of Crowd Pleaser, an events and performances company committed to championing creators in cabaret & variety.  

She absolutely loves the RPG afternoons with Dan, Tony, Teddy & the crew, which. led to her being an unhinged voice on "Tales beneath the surface."
