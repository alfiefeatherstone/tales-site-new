Tales Beneath the Surface is a collection of original horror stories set in a version of London where the ordinary and the uncanny overlap. Familiar streets, flats and Tube platforms are never quite what they seem.

Each episode features several self-contained tales, narrated with dark humour and unsettling charm by The Narrator. It is made for fans of anthology podcasts and atmospheric storytelling.
